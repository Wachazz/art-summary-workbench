What ART clinical features are essential for summarising HIV clinical reports?

Eight features were tracked: regimen, viral load, CD4, adherence, TB screen, allergy, comorbidity and next appointment. The bars show how many sentences carry each feature across the 30 synthetic records. Input size after the feature guided selector

BART reads at most 1,024 tokens. The synthetic notes are short, so they fit anyway. Real multi year records are longer, and the selector keeps the model input inside the limit.

Pipeline

  1. 1

    Corpus audit. Count usable records, check completeness and length before anything is trained.

  2. 2

    De-identification. Names, card numbers and addresses removed before data leaves the clinic.

  3. 3

    Patient level split. All records of one patient stay in one set, so no patient is seen in training and in testing.

  4. 4

    Feature guided selector. Keeps the sentences that carry ART features, so the input fits the 1,024 token limit of BART.

  5. 5

    Fine tune. BART base, with BioBART base as the alternative checkpoint.

  6. 6

    Evaluate. Three layers, against a template and a TextRank baseline.

Corpus audit gate, 18 December 2026. The decision on silver or gold references and on model size is taken here, on what the audit finds.

Aims

  • Features. Identify the essential ART features a clinician needs in a summary.
  • Model. Develop and fine tune the summarisation model.
  • Utility. Assess clinical utility with clinicians.

Three evaluation layers

  • Overlap: ROUGE 1, 2, L and BERTScore against the reference.
  • Factual audit: each key fact in the summary checked against the source record.
  • Clinician ratings: accuracy, completeness and usefulness scored by clinicians.

Learning curve

Train on 25, 50, 75 and 100 percent of the training patients to show whether more data still helps.

Clinician crossover on review time

Each clinician reviews two sets of records, one with the full record and one with the model summary, in opposite order across two groups. Review time is analysed with a mixed model on the log of time, with clinician as a random effect. Comprehension is tested for non inferiority.

Ethics and data

The study needs Medical Research Council of Zimbabwe approval before any real record is touched. Theory frame: Cognitive Load Theory for why a summary should cut review effort, and UTAUT for whether clinicians would adopt it.

What is in the folder

  • generate_data.py makes 30 synthetic ART records with a seed.
  • summarizers.py template, TextRank, feature guided selector and the BART wrapper.
  • evaluate.py ROUGE 1, 2, L and the key fact check.
  • run_pipeline.py runs every system and writes the results this page shows.
  • train_bart.py fine tunes BART or BioBART with a patient level split.
  • app.py small Flask server for live summaries.
